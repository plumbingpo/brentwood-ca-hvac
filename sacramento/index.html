Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Sacramento

A little routine care is the difference between a system that runs for years and one that breaks down in the middle of a heat wave. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a new filter every one to three months, at least two feet of open space around the outdoor condenser, a spring rinse of the condenser coil, and two professional tune-ups a year, so small issues get caught early.

The age of your equipment matters just as much. With regular service, a central air conditioner usually runs 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts acting up, like rising energy bills, rooms that won't reach the setpoint, or repeat service calls, it's time to plan a replacement. A planned replacement is cheaper and is far less stressful than an emergency swap during the worst heat wave of summer.

The earlier you report a comfort problem, the cheaper it usually is. A weak capacitor costs little to replace, yet that same capacitor, ignored for a season, can ruin the compressor and force a full system replacement. Save our number on your phone today so help is one call away as soon as something seems off in your home. A quick call saves time, money, and sleepless nights.

Comfort Challenges Sacramento Homeowners Face

HVAC systems in this area face a familiar set of problems.
